## 1. Founder & Business Context

- Tanim is the founder and sole operator of **SlideIn Venture**, a B2B content production and cold outreach service. Running it in parallel with university (business administration, Bangladesh). Freelancing since 2021, originally as a video editor, expanding into cold outreach and AI automation.
- SlideIn Venture's existing service line: end-to-end podcast/video editing, short-form clip repurposing (8–10 clips/episode), and manual cold email outreach infrastructure.
- Existing ICP framework (already in use for SlideIn): consultants, coaches, fractional executives. Skip criteria — consumer lifestyle creators, generic aliases, hyper-local small businesses, large corporations without reachable decision-makers, non-profits.
- **This new venture (the 100-Appointment Guarantee) targets the same ICP**, repackaged into a single high-ticket, guaranteed-outcome sprint instead of ongoing service-line retainers.
- Past lesson learned (from earlier SlideIn GTM work, worth remembering here): a previous lead list underperformed because the pitch led with the most commoditized service (editing) instead of the differentiated concept. **Do not repeat this mistake with the new offer — lead with the guarantee and the outcome, never with "cold outreach" or "video editing" as the headline.**

## 2. The Offer: The 100-Appointment Guarantee

**Name:** The 100-Appointment Guarantee
**Price:** $9,999 flat fee, 90 days *(see Section 6 — recommended reprice to $12,500–14,999 for the next cohort)*
**Core promise:** 100 booked appointments in 90 days, or Tanim keeps working for free until the number is hit.

### Offer stack (named, valued components)
| Component | Description | Standalone market value |
|---|---|---|
| Inbox Authority Infrastructure | Enterprise-grade sending setup — SES, DNS/SPF/DKIM/DMARC, domain warm-up, deliverability monitoring | $4,000 |
| AI-Matched Outreach Engine | ICP-scored dossiers, personalized cold email at volume, run 90 days | $7,500 |
| Branded Conversion Funnel | Custom landing page → email capture → nurture → booking | $2,000 |
| Agent Ops Dashboard | Not a Notion board — an agent-driven command center that executes tasks and logs everything | $3,500 |
| 90-Video Content Engine | Scripted, produced, distributed across platforms; client picks 1 of 4 production paths (see below) | $9,000 |
| Paid Social Amplification | Ads run against the video content for 90 days | $4,000 |
| White-Glove Onboarding System | Intake form, T&Cs, week-1/week-2 breakdown, walkthrough videos | $750 |
| **Total stacked value** | | **~$30,750** |

Honest note: this is ~3:1 value-to-price, not the textbook 10:1. Don't fake the gap — instead anchor the pitch against the *Dream Outcome* (100 appointments × even a 10% close rate on a $10K deal = $100K+ pipeline), which is a legitimate 10:1+ argument.

### Video production paths (client chooses one)
1. Client shoots their own footage, Tanim scripts it
2. AI avatar / voice clone of the client, Tanim produces and runs ads against it — **recommended default/flagship path, zero client effort**
3. AI persona/influencer acts as brand representative
4. Real person (client) on camera, Tanim shoots and edits 90 reels

**Decision:** Lead the pitch with Path 2 or 3 (zero client effort). Frame Paths 1 and 4 as accommodations, not the default — every unit of client effort lowers the Value Equation (Dream Outcome × Likelihood ÷ Time × Effort).

### Guarantee — fine print **[OPEN — needs to be written into the onboarding doc before this goes live]**
"100 Booked Appointments Guarantee": if fewer than 100 qualified appointments are booked in 90 days, Tanim keeps working free until the number is hit. Must define in writing, before signing:
- What counts as a "qualified appointment" (booked via the landing page, matching agreed ICP)
- No-shows caused by the client don't count against Tanim
- The 90-day clock pauses if the client blocks copy/creative approval for 48+ hours

### Scarcity (real, not manufactured)
Solo operator, bespoke infra per client (dedicated sending domains, custom dashboard, 90 videos each) — genuine capacity ceiling. **Cap: 4 new clients per quarter.** Framing: shared infrastructure across too many clients degrades deliverability for everyone on it.

### Post-sprint retention offer
$500/month maintenance retainer after the 90-day sprint — keeps the infrastructure running, no new deliverables promised.

## 3. Blue Ocean Positioning

You're straddling three red oceans at once (cold email agencies / video-content agencies / AI dashboard tools). Nobody bundles all three into one guaranteed fixed-fee sprint — that bundle is the blue ocean, but only if pitched as a category of one, not as "email + video + dashboard sold separately."

### ERRC Grid
- **Eliminate:** open-ended monthly retainer as entry point; client writing their own scripts; requirement to appear on camera; generic templated copy; disconnected tools (spreadsheets/Notion)
- **Reduce:** sales-call friction (onboarding video does the selling); time-to-first-booked-call; client's day-to-day involvement
- **Raise:** inbox placement/deliverability quality; personalization depth; content volume (90 vs. industry's 8–10); transparency (everything logged live in the dashboard)
- **Create:** an *agent* dashboard that executes tasks, not just displays them; client's choice of 4 video production paths; one flat-fee sprint bundling acquisition infra + content + ops; a hard-number performance guarantee instead of vague "leads"

### Non-customer tiers to target
1. **Soon-to-be:** consultants already running their own Instantly/Smartlead campaigns, hitting spam-folder issues, ready to outsource
2. **Refusing:** consultants burned before by generic lead-gen agencies (bought lists, templated spam) — win them with the dashboard's transparency + the named guarantee
3. **Unexplored (biggest untapped pool):** coaches who've only grown via referrals/organic content and assume outbound is "spammy" or beneath their brand — reframe the whole pitch as a personal-brand/content upgrade that happens to generate pipeline, and lead with the 90 videos, not the cold outreach

## 4. Market Research Summary (as of August 2026)

### Market size
- Global coaching market: $5.34B (2025) → $5.8B projected 2026, 8.5–17% CAGR depending on segment (ICF / IBISWorld)
- Executive coaching/leadership development: $112.98B in 2026, up from $103.56B in 2025
- US business coaching alone: $20.0B market (IBISWorld, 2026)
- ~122,974 active coach practitioners globally (ICF), growing ~17% YoY; plus a much larger pool of independent consultants and fractional execs
- SOM (your 1,500-lead list specifically): realistically **2–15 new clients** from this list at current cold-email reply-rate benchmarks (industry avg reply rate has fallen to ~3.4% in 2026) — the list is precious, don't burn it on an unproven message

### Competitive pricing benchmarks
- B2B appointment-setting agencies: $3,000–$10,000/month retainer, or $150–$500 per qualified/ICP-matched meeting (DemandNexus, 2026 benchmarks)
- AI SDR tools: $500–$5,000+/month, still require human oversight — "deploy AI, remove humans" has underperformed industry-wide
- Freelance video editing: $1,500–$3,000/month for a cadence now largely replicable by $15–100/month AI tools (Argil, Cometly benchmarks)
- **Takeaway:** your $9,999 price is well below the ~$30-50K market value of 100 ICP-matched appointments alone — room to raise price, not cut it

### AI-resilience findings (task-level)
- Raw cold-email sending/sequencing: ~95% replaceable by AI today (SaaStr operator data) — a genuinely commoditized task
- Deliverability infrastructure/architecture: getting *more* valuable, not less — AI SDR volume increases the need for domain/warm-up/monitoring engineering
- Content production/video editing: heavily tooled and compressed in price by AI
- Generalist "I'll do your marketing tasks" positioning: the most exposed category — agencies selling "we do everything" are packaging replaceable tasks, and are losing clients to fee-compression as clients use the same AI tools to negotiate agencies down
- **Conclusion:** the 100-Appointment Guarantee survives because it charges for a guaranteed outcome backed by infrastructure and judgment — not for raw hours of sending emails or editing clips.

## 5. Offer B — Parked (why, and what to salvage)

Offer B was the generalist cold-email pitch: *"I can handle about 80% of your work... content production to reaching out to the right people... I'll keep working until you actually start getting more clients."*

**Decision: do not pitch Offer B as a standalone product.** It has no price, no defined scope, no end condition — the exact shape of offer the 2026 market data says is most exposed to AI-driven fee compression and client self-service.

**What to keep from it:** the email's *tone and structure* is genuinely good — humble, low-pressure, two response paths (quick call or 90-second video). Reuse that wrapper for the 100-Appointment Guarantee pitch. Swap the vague offer inside it for the named, priced, guaranteed one.

## 6. Pricing & Revenue Model

**Goal:** $20,000–30,000/month.

| | Offer A as-is ($9,999, 4/quarter cap) | Offer A repriced ($12,500–14,999, same cap) |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly from new sales alone | ~$13,300 | ~$16,700–20,000 |
| Path to $20–30K | Needs the $500/mo retainer layer stacking on top (5–10 past clients = $2,500–5,000/mo) | Gets there faster; still far below the $30–50K market value of 100 appointments alone |

**Decision:** reprice the *next* cohort to $12,500–14,999. Keep the 4-per-quarter cap — it's a real capacity constraint (bespoke infra + 90 videos per client), not manufactured scarcity, so don't increase headcount without adding help.
