# The 100-Appointment Loop

# Process & Deliverables Map

---

## Phase 0 — Positioning & Sales Assets *(built once, reused on every pitch, before any client is locked)*

1. Category-defining one-liner distancing from "appointment setting" or "content agency"
2. Named, branded components for every pitch (e.g. "Command Center," "Buyer Radar," "The 90-Day Booked Call Guarantee")
3. Bounded, defined guarantee — capped extension, "qualified" logged 3 ways
4. Live technical audit performed on the sales call itself as the opener
5. Proof-of-concept mini-build used as the live demo (tool suite or video personalization shown live)
6. Founder-market-fit narrative (video/marketing background into this build)
## Phase 1 — Client Locked: ICP Research & Foundation

1. Documented Ideal Client Profile for the business
2. Ranked list of the types of people/companies to reach out to
3. Reachable-market size estimate
4. List of platforms/communities where those prospects actually are
5. Channel-viability scorecard specific to this client
6. Lookalike list from the client's existing best customers
*Everything below in Phases 2–6 fans out and runs at the same time from here.*

## Phase 2A — Content Sub-Agent: Real-Footage Path **(parallel)**

1. 90-day script/topic calendar
2. Shot list/script outline the client films from
3. Filming guide (lighting, framing, audio basics)
4. Raw footage intake and organization system
5. Edited, captioned, platform-formatted video files
6. Thumbnail set per video
7. Posting/publishing execution across platforms
8. AI-assisted rough cut, your final touch only
9. Weekly content-performance digest tied to booked calls
10. Tagged, searchable video asset library
## Phase 2B — Content Sub-Agent: AI Avatar Path **(parallel, alternate to 2A)**

1. AI avatar built from the client's likeness
2. AI voice clone of the client
3. Script library the avatar delivers from
4. Modular script system, one narrative remixed across formats
5. Rendered 90-video AI-avatar batch
6. AI-content disclosure/labeling applied per platform
7. Signed likeness/voice consent form before any avatar work starts
## Phase 2C — Client's Own Sales/Pitch Video **(parallel)**

1. Script/talking-points outline for the pitch video
2. Filmed and edited final version
3. Cutdowns for site, funnel, and outreach use
## Phase 3 — Micro-SaaS Free Tool Suite **(parallel; feeds Phase 4's funnel)**

1. One reusable tool-suite engine, re-themed per client instead of rebuilt
2. 3–8 genuinely useful free tools per client
3. Tools matched to what the client actually sells (calculator, estimator, checker)
4. Email-gate placed after the result, not before
5. Usage-based lead scoring (which tool they used signals what they want)
6. White-labeled under the client's branded domain
7. A "power tool" flagship — the one tool people actually share
8. Analytics on which tool converts best, fed back into the dashboard
9. Freemium upsell path from tool user to booked call
10. Your own tool suite live on your business before selling it to anyone
## Phase 4 — Lead-Capture Website & Funnel **(parallel — "the bait")**

1. Purpose-built funnel site on a separate branded domain
2. Embedded booking calendar
3. Free interactive quiz tied to the offer
4. Free calculator/estimator tool
5. Instant quote/pricing-estimate tool
6. "You vs the market" comparison tool
7. Visual listing/showcase page as a passive magnet
8. Gated downloadable guide or checklist
9. Free mini-audit/assessment
10. Webinar/live-demo registration page
11. "Unlock your results" teaser page
12. Exit-intent popup
13. Case studies/testimonial section
14. Consent/cookie banner on every page
15. Sub-60-second speed-to-lead automation on every form fill
16. Mobile-optimized, fast-loading build
17. Analytics and pixel tracking installed
## Phase 5 — Lead List Building, Research & Scoring **(parallel)**

1. Master prospect list matched to the ICP
2. Verified email address per prospect
3. Per-lead research dossier (role, company, recent activity)
4. "Why this lead matches the ICP" note per lead
5. Personalization hook per lead
6. Lead source tag (organic / funnel / cold)
7. Lead score against the client's actual close-rate data
8. Cleaned, deduplicated master lead sheet / CRM import
9. Dead-lead recycling (re-score and re-approach at 90 days)
10. Buying-committee mapping for multi-stakeholder deals
11. Research-completion tracker across thousands of leads
## Phase 6 — Signal-Based / Deanonymization Intelligence **(parallel; feeds Phases 5, 7, 8)**

1. Website visitor deanonymization setup (Warmly/Bullseye-style, B2B)
2. Deanonymization setup for B2C/DTC traffic ([Retention.com](https://Retention.com)-style)
3. Real-time hot-lead alert when a named account hits a key page
4. Trigger-event monitoring (funding, hiring, launch) feeding outreach
5. Auto-generated "why now" note per triggered lead
6. Non-creepy signal-use playbook baked into outreach copy
7. Timing-tested outreach cadence, A/B'd per client
8. Free-tool-suite usage as its own trigger signal
9. Competitive-displacement signal tracking
10. Micro-SaaS-to-CRM auto-routing
*Phases 2–6 converge here: content is live, leads are researched, funnels are catching traffic, signals are firing. Outreach starts.*

## Phase 7 — Cold Email Infrastructure & Outreach Execution

1. Dedicated outreach domain(s), separate from the client's main domain
2. SPF, DKIM, DMARC configuration
3. Mailbox warm-up schedule
4. Multi-step personalized cold email sequence built from the "why now" note
5. Follow-up sequence architecture (4–7 touches)
6. Small, verified-batch sending instead of one giant blast
7. Reply-sentiment classification with routing
8. Reply-handling and objection-response templates
9. Suppression list and opt-out system
10. Deliverability/spam-rate monitoring report
## Phase 8 — AI Video Personalization Outreach

1. AI-personalized video outreach at scale (name/company/site auto-inserted)
2. Personalized video thumbnails per prospect
3. Sub-60-second video-message scripting for outreach
4. Video embedded directly in the first-touch email
5. ABM-style named-account video landing pages for top prospects
6. Video-engagement analytics feeding the lead score
## Phase 9 — LinkedIn / Social Presence & Alternative Channels **(runs continuously alongside outreach)**

1. LinkedIn copywriting and posting on the client's page
2. Facebook business page content and posting
3. Instagram business page content and posting
4. LinkedIn connection + DM outreach campaign
5. Comment-to-DM funnel off the client's own posts
6. Keyword-triggered DM automation
7. Cross-platform repurposing pipeline from one source asset
8. Follower/engagement tracking per platform
9. Partner/affiliate referral funnel
10. Community engagement funnel (FB groups, Discord, Reddit)
11. Podcast guesting/interview swap
12. Local SEO/Google Business Profile funnel
13. Co-branded webinar with a partner's audience
14. QR-code offline-to-online funnel
15. SMS opt-in funnel from existing contacts
16. Free tool suite shared as a standalone viral asset
## Phase 10 — Website Chatbot & AI Voice Receptionist **(live before outreach starts, catches inbound the whole time)**

1. Website chatbot scoped to one job: qualify and book
2. Chatbot that opens by identifying itself as AI
3. Chatbot embedded on the free tool suite itself, not just the main site
4. Knowledge base built strictly from the client's actual offer/FAQs
5. Chatbot escalation to a human when it's stuck
6. Inbound/callback-only AI voice receptionist (no proactive dialing — TCPA)
7. Chat-only fallback for clients who skip voice AI
8. Call transcription and summary log
## Phase 11 — Branded Domain & Email Infrastructure **(set up early, referenced throughout)**

1. New domain purchased and themed to the client's brand
2. Professional email addresses set up under that domain
3. Access/permissions structure — you retain admin
4. Booking restricted to route only through those managed emails
5. Email signature and branding templates
6. Documented offboarding policy for domain/email/dashboard
## Phase 12 — Hyper-Personalized Pre-Call Research **(triggers the moment a lead engages or books)**

1. Refreshed research dossier once a call is booked
2. One-page "who you're about to talk to" brief, auto-compiled
3. Suggested talking points and likely objections per prospect
4. Meeting-outcome log tied back to the original research
## Phase 13 — Agentic Dashboard / Command Center **(live from day one, runs underneath every phase above)**

1. Dashboard instance provisioned and branded for the client
2. Upcoming-meetings module
3. Lead-list-with-context module
4. Automations module (what's running in the background)
5. Assets module (content, domains, credentials, tool suite)
6. ICP module
7. Real-time lead-status timeline
8. Guarantee-tracking ledger with timestamped proof of delivery
9. Auto-generated weekly narrative report
10. Plain-language query interface ("show me who mentioned budget this week")
11. Client-facing login, always available
## Phase 14 — Meeting Booking, Confirmation & Show-Rate

1. Single booking link tied to a controlled email
2. 24hr + 4hr automated confirmation/reminder sequence
3. Pre-meeting value-reinforcement video sent before the call
4. One-click reschedule link in every reminder
5. Qualify-before-booking gate
6. No-show automatic recovery/rebooking flow
7. Real-time booked-meeting count log visible to both sides
## Phase 15 — Post-Meeting: Reporting & Guarantee Tracking

1. Qualified-appointment definition logged 3 ways (budget, need-fit, confirmed slot)
2. Weekly/monthly performance report
3. Cost-per-booked-meeting tracked by channel
4. Show-rate reported separately from booked-rate
5. Approval-delay clock-pause logic built into the guarantee timeline
6. Post-mortem framework for underperforming channels
## Phase 16 — Compliance & Consent **(threaded through every phase above, not a one-time step)**

1. Signed likeness/voice consent form for avatar/clone use
2. AI-disclosure/consent framework across every channel
3. Data privacy/consent-capture policy per funnel and form
4. Terms/consent language on the free tool suite specifically
5. Vendor-risk review for core tools (Warmly, Bullseye, etc.)
6. Contract clause defining post-engagement ownership of domain/data/dashboard
## Phase 17 — After the Guarantee Period: Retention & Recurring Revenue

1. Flat monthly retainer to keep the whole system running post-guarantee
2. Client health-score model flagging churn risk
3. Renewal/expansion proposal auto-generated at guarantee end
4. Quarterly business review deck assembled from dashboard data
5. Automated referral-request trigger after a good outcome
## Phase 18 — Beyond This Offer: High-Demand Expansion *(not part of the default build, real market demand if you widen scope)*

1. Multi-touch attribution model setup
2. CRM migration and historical data cleanup
3. API integration map across the client's existing tools
4. White-label version of the whole system resold through other agencies
5. Review-generation/reputation-management funnel
6. Paid retargeting audit for clients who want ads alongside the organic system
7. Landing-page A/B testing as an ongoing service
---

**172 deliverables across 19 phases**, ordered the way they actually happen in your process rather than by abstract category: positioning → ICP → parallel fan-out (content, tools, funnel, leads, signals) → outreach → social presence → chatbot/voice → domain/email → pre-call research → dashboard (running underneath the whole time) → booking → guarantee tracking → compliance (threaded throughout) → retention → expansion.
