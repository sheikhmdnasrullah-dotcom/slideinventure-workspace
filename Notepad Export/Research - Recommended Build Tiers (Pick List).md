# Recommended Build Tiers — The 100-Appointment Loop

Synthesis of: The 100-Appointment Loop.md (172 items / 19 phases), The Plan.md (positioning, blue ocean, pricing, legal corrections), and four 2026 market-research passes (Micro-SaaS Tool Suite, Deanonymization + AI Video Outreach, AI Chatbot + Voice Receptionist, Agentic Client Dashboard — all in this folder).

**v2 — updated after your decisions on content path, tool suite, voice AI, and pricing.** Four forks below are marked DECIDED. Everything else is still a proposal to discuss, not final.

## How to read the tiers

- **CORE** — build for every client from day one. Validated demand, solo-buildable, low risk.
- **T2** — add once you're running more than one client and the core loop is proven.
- **LATER** — real, but only makes sense at team/multi-client scale.
- **CUT** — not part of the offer. Either genuinely low-value here, or a deliberate positioning choice.

---

## Decisions locked this round

1. **Content production — equal menu of all 4 paths (DECIDED).** Not defaulting to real-footage. Client picks A (films it themselves) / B (AI avatar) / C (synthetic AI rep) / D (you shoot it) freely, per "Items I will provide.md." Worth keeping in view even though the call stands: AI-avatar content runs ~half the CTR of real UGC (0.9–1.3% vs 1.8–2.4%) and converts 20–25% lower on retargeting per The Plan.md's research — that's a real tradeoff for whichever client picks path B, not a reason to remove the option.
2. **Micro-SaaS tool suite — premium add-on only (DECIDED).** Not in the default bundle. Build one flagship tool for your own business first; offer the full suite as a paid upsell once the engine exists and you have more than one client.
3. **AI voice receptionist — cut entirely (DECIDED).** Not offered, not even as an opt-in. Your call, and it's the right one for this positioning specifically: voice-AI-for-inbound-calls is a saturated, commodity category in 2026 (Retell, Vapi, Synthflow, Bland all selling the same thing), and it works against — not for — a "we don't just sell activity like everyone else" category position. The chatbot stays (Voiceflow-based, qualify-and-book scoped, self-identifies as AI) since that layer is still validated and low-risk. Phase 10 below reflects this.
4. **Guarantee & pricing — monthly retainer, not a project-then-guarantee model (DECIDED).** Replacing the original "$5k for 50 / $10k for 100, then it's over" structure with **$5,000/month, targeting 40–50 qualified meetings/month, ongoing.** This is a real fix for the biggest structural problem in the original sketch (The Plan.md's Correction 1: no recurring revenue anywhere in the old model) — the retainer isn't something you pitch *after* a guarantee period ends, it *is* the offer from day one. Full breakdown below in the rewritten Guarantee & Pricing section — including one number worth seeing before you lock it in.

## Guarantee & Pricing — rewritten for the monthly retainer model

**$5,000/month, targeting 40–50 qualified meetings/month, ongoing** (per decision #4). This solves The Plan.md's Correction 1 directly — recurring revenue exists from the first invoice, not as a pitch you make after a 90-day guarantee period ends.

**One number worth seeing clearly before this gets quoted to anyone:** $5,000 ÷ 40–50 meetings = **$100–125 per qualified meeting.** The Plan.md's own market research (Part 4, sources listed there) found the 2026 floor for pay-per-appointment agencies at $150–400/meeting, and $400–750 for BANT-verified appointments. This price still sits below that floor. That's not a reason to override your decision — it may be the right call deliberately, since you're not selling per-appointment like a call center, you're selling the whole installed system (content + research + outreach + dashboard) at a flat rate, which is exactly the "sell an outcome, not activity" positioning from The Plan.md Part 1. But it's worth being a deliberate choice rather than an accidental one — options if you want to revisit later: raise the flat fee once you have 1–2 case studies, or keep it flat as a below-market anchor specifically to win the first few clients fast.

**Still open, not yet decided — flagging rather than assuming:**
- **Qualified-meeting definition:** carry over The Plan.md's 3-way definition (budget/authority confirmed + need-fit confirmed + confirmed date/time on calendar), logged in the dashboard so it's never a debate after the fact. No reason to change this with the new pricing model.
- **Shortfall mechanic:** if a month lands under 40, what happens — rolls into next month's target, refund the gap, or just reported transparently with no penalty since it's a retainer rather than a capped project guarantee? This wasn't specified yet and changes the risk math meaningfully; worth deciding before the first client contract.
- **Minimum term / cancellation:** month-to-month, or a minimum commitment (e.g. 3 months) to make the retainer relationship worth the setup cost on your side?
