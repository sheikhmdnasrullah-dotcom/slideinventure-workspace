# The ICP

**High-profile personal-brand experts and consultants with a $2,000+ core offer, an existing audience, and no real system turning attention into booked calls.**

Using the starving-crowd filter (pain, purchasing power, targetability, growth):

- **Pain:** they get inbound interest (DMs, comments, replies) but follow-up is manual and calls leak through the cracks. This is the same "speed to lead" pain from your real estate playbook, just showing up as a comment thread instead of a phone line.
- **Purchasing power:** a $2k+ program means one closed client covers your $5k-$10k fee several times over, same logic as the real estate agent's $9k commission math.
- **Targetability:** "high profile" means they already publish somewhere (podcast, YouTube, LinkedIn, newsletter), which makes them easy to research and feeds your Loom-first demo play and the deanonymization/research engine directly.
- **Growth:** the creator-led expert economy is still expanding into 2026, and none of these people think of themselves as "small business," so they don't flinch at your price the way a median agent would.
Avoid brand-new coaches with no audience or revenue yet. No content presence means no fit for the content engine, and no revenue means no purchasing power.
