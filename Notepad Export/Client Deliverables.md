# Client Deliverables

**Phase 1: Client Locked, ICP Research**

1. Documented Ideal Client Profile for their business
2. A ranked list of the types of people/companies to reach out to
3. A reachable-market size estimate
4. A list of platforms/communities where those prospects actually are
5. A content and outreach angle brief built from that ICP
**Phase 2: Content Sub-Agent (90 videos)**

1. A 90-day script/topic list for their business
2. If they can film: a simple shot list/filming guide they follow
3. If they can film: 90 edited, captioned videos posted to Instagram/Facebook
4. If they can't film: their own AI avatar built from their likeness
5. If they can't film: their own AI voice clone
6. 90 AI-avatar videos scripted, generated, and posted in their place
7. Signed consent form covering use of their likeness/voice
8. Captions, hashtags, and a posting schedule handled for every video
9. A weekly content performance summary (what's getting traction)
**Phase 3: Lead List Sub-Agent (runs in parallel)**

1. A master prospect list matched to the ICP
2. A verified email address for every prospect on it
3. A personal-context note per lead (what they do, what they post about)
4. A "why this lead fits your ICP" note per lead
5. A "recent activity" note per lead to use as the personalization hook
6. A cleaned, deduplicated lead sheet, built out to 2,000-3,000 leads
7. A source tag per lead (which funnel or channel it came from)
**Phase 4: Lead Capture Funnels (the "bait" into the website)**

1. A free interactive quiz tied to their offer, gated by email
2. A free calculator or estimator tool tied to their offer
3. A visual listing/showcase page that gates "see more" behind an email
4. A gated downloadable guide or checklist
5. An Instagram/Facebook comment-to-DM automation funneling commenters to the site
6. A DM auto-responder that delivers the lead magnet and captures the email
7. A free audit/assessment funnel
8. A webinar or live-demo signup page
9. A "share to unlock" referral-gated resource
10. A LinkedIn post-to-website funnel using the same lead magnet
11. An instant quote/pricing-estimate tool
12. A community-engagement funnel (posting value where prospects already gather, linking back)
**Phase 5: Email Infrastructure**

1. Outreach domain(s) purchased separately from their main domain
2. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC configured on those domains
3. A mailbox warm-up schedule run before any sending starts
4. A deliverability monitoring report (bounce rate, spam rate)
5. A written, multi-step cold email sequence built from the lead-context notes
6. Personalized opening lines generated per lead from that context
7. Reply-handling templates for the common response types
8. An unsubscribe/suppression system
**Phase 6: The Client's Own Sales Video**

1. A script/talking-points outline for their pitch video
2. The filmed and edited version of that video
3. Cut-down versions for the website, funnel, and email
**Phase 7: Marketing Website + Agentic Chatbot**

1. A fully built marketing/lead-capturing website
2. Booking calendar embedded on the site
3. The lead-capture bait tool embedded on the site
4. An AI chatbot that answers questions and qualifies visitors
5. The chatbot's knowledge base built from their actual offer and FAQs
6. An AI voice receptionist on the site or phone line, only if they want one
7. A documented fallback where the chatbot handles that job if they skip voice AI
8. Mobile-optimized, fast-loading build
9. Analytics and tracking installed on the site
**Phase 8: Hyper-Personalized Research (background agents)**

1. A full background dossier per lead once they're in the 2-3k list
2. A social-activity summary per lead
3. A role/company/professional background summary per lead
4. A ready-to-use personalization hook per lead
5. Refreshed research per lead as new information surfaces
6. A research-completion tracker so nothing gets missed across thousands of leads
**Phase 9: The Agentic Dashboard**

1. A dashboard instance built and branded for the client
2. An "upcoming meetings" module
3. A "lead list with context" module
4. An "automations" module showing what's running in the background
5. An "assets" module (content, domains, credentials, all in one place)
6. An "ICP" module documenting who they're targeting and why
7. A real-time lead-status timeline
8. A meeting-outcome log per completed call
9. A client login so they can check their own dashboard anytime
10. A running count of booked meetings against the guarantee
**Phase 10: The Separate Branded Domain & Email (you control access)**

1. A new domain purchased and themed to match their business
2. That domain built out as the actual funnel site
3. Professional email addresses set up under that domain
4. Booking restricted to only route through those managed emails
5. A documented access/permissions structure
6. A transparency log showing exactly how many calls came through
**Phase 11: Pre-Call Prep & Booking**

1. A one-page "who you're about to talk to" brief before every call
2. That brief pulled straight from the dashboard for the client to read
3. Automated calendar invite and confirmation to the prospect
4. A reminder sequence to cut no-shows
5. A rebooking flow triggered automatically after a no-show
**Phase 12: LinkedIn / Facebook / Instagram Presence**

1. A LinkedIn content calendar for their page
2. Written LinkedIn posts published on their behalf
3. Written Facebook business page posts published on their behalf
4. Written Instagram captions to run alongside the video content
5. A consistent cross-platform posting schedule
6. A follower/engagement tracker per platform
**Phase 13: Video Editing & Organization (support layer, kept lean)**

1. Every video the client records for site, funnel, or sales use, edited and organized
2. An AI-assisted rough-cut pass on every video before your final touch, so it takes less of your time per video
3. A tagged, searchable video asset library so nothing gets lost or reshot
4. Long-form and short-form cutdowns of every video produced
**Phase 14: After the Meeting Is Booked**

1. The booked meeting logged automatically into the dashboard
2. The pre-call brief archived with the meeting record
3. The call outcome logged back into the dashboard afterward
4. No-show or reschedule automatically triggering the recovery flow
5. A running report comparing meetings booked against the guaranteed number
6. A weekly or monthly performance report for the client
7. A renewal/retainer proposal generated once the guarantee period ends
**Running Throughout**

1. A documented SOP so the process can be handed to a contractor
2. A client-responsibility checklist (what the client still has to provide, like their own footage)
3. A contract clause defining what happens to the domain, emails, and dashboard if the client leaves
4. A consent and disclosure policy covering the AI avatar, AI voice, and AI outreach
