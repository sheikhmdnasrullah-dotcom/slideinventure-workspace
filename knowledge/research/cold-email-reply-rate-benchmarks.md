## What this is

Third-party benchmark data pulled from web research (Woodpecker, Cleanlist,
InboxKit, Instantly — see `source`), not SlideIn Venture's own send data.
No campaign has been run and measured against these numbers yet, so this
is context to calibrate expectations against, not a validated target.
`ai_inferred`, not `confirmed`.

## Headline numbers

- Overall average reply rate: **~3.1–3.4%** across 2026 benchmark reports.
- Top-quartile campaigns: **5.5%**. Elite/top-performer campaigns: **8–12%**.
- Bottom performers: **below 0.5%**.
- Trend line is declining year over year: 8.5% (2019) → 7% (2023) → ~5%
  (2025) → 3–5% entering 2026 — attributed to inbox saturation and
  stronger spam filtering, not just worse copywriting.

## Campaign size — directly relevant to how SlideIn Venture sends

- Campaigns **under 50 recipients**: ~5.8% reply rate.
- Campaigns **500+ recipients**: ~2.1% reply rate.

This is the one number worth flagging against `sop-2026-08-18-001`
directly: the existing cold email SOP is built around small-batch,
one-detail-per-email personalization (real detail from the recipient's
content, no generic subject lines), which structurally matches the
smaller-campaign end of this benchmark — not the mass-blast end. If true,
the SOP's approach is aligned with what the external data says works, but
this hasn't been checked against SlideIn Venture's own reply data yet.

## Personalization factors

- Personalized emails: **~32% higher response rate** than generic.
- Customized subject lines: **~50% higher open rate**.
- Verified email lists: **~2x** the reply rate of unverified lists.

These three line up with existing SOP rules (specific real detail in the
opener, 3–7 word personalized subject line) without contradicting them.

## Industry variation (context, not SlideIn-specific)

Legal services reportedly lead at ~10% response rate; IT services lag at
~3.5%. No creator/content-agency-specific figure surfaced in this search —
open question below.

## Open questions

- No benchmark source broke out reply rates specifically for
  content-creator or video-production outreach — the closest proxy here is
  the campaign-size data, not an industry-matched number.
- SlideIn Venture has no internal send data yet to compare against these
  external figures.

## Recommendation

Use ~3–5% as a rough sanity floor and ~5.5%+ as a reasonable stretch
target once real campaigns are running, but treat both as external
priors, not SlideIn Venture-specific facts, until measured. Founder should
review and flip to `confirmed` only after real campaign data exists to
check against.
